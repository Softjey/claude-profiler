  derivable from summing `message.usage` (FR15's stated method). The session has 919

  139,992,949, and no transformation of the raw transcript reproduces it exactly.
  Per user decision, the criterion in `plan.md` was corrected to describe what FR15 actually
  specifies (raw sum, reproducible in 100% of sessions) instead of the internal,
  non-reproducible `cost-state` figure. See `plan.md`'s T7 acceptance criteria for the
  full rationale, kept there for future readers.
